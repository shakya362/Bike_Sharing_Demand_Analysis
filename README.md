📌 Project Overview

Bike Sharing Demand Analysis is a data analytics project focused on understanding bike rental demand and identifying the major factors that influence usage patterns.

The project uses historical bike-sharing data to explore relationships between time, seasonality, weather conditions, working days, holidays, and rental demand.

The objective is to transform raw data into meaningful business insights that can help bike-sharing businesses improve:

🚲 Fleet availability
📊 Demand forecasting
🌦️ Weather-based planning
⏰ Peak-hour operations
📍 Resource allocation
📈 Business decision-making

Bike-sharing demand is strongly influenced by temporal and environmental factors, making exploratory analysis valuable for understanding usage patterns and operational requirements.

🎯 Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

Understand bike rental demand patterns.
Clean and prepare the dataset for analysis.
Perform exploratory data analysis.
Identify important demand-driving factors.
Analyze seasonal and time-based trends.
Understand the impact of weather conditions.
Create meaningful visualizations and dashboards.
Generate actionable business insights.
Present the findings in a clear and professional format.

📈 Expected Insights

Typical bike-sharing analysis shows strong temporal patterns, including differences between commute periods on working days and more distributed recreational demand during weekends. Weather and seasonal conditions can also materially affect demand.

The analysis can therefore support operational decisions around:

🚲 Fleet Management

Increase bike availability during high-demand periods.

⏰ Peak-Hour Planning

Identify periods where additional operational capacity may be required.

🌦️ Weather-Based Planning

Use weather conditions as an input when planning expected demand.

📅 Seasonal Strategy

Adjust fleet distribution and operational planning according to seasonal demand.
